Core System Architecture

Resolving the compound problem of peak commuter surge and competing commercial delivery footprints.

Pillar 1: Commuter Transit

Autonomous High-Capacity Transit (BRT) & Feeders

Overcoming the limitations of traditional fixed-route busing by dynamically aligning capacity to passenger volume while eliminating operational driver shift overheads.

  • 1
    Arterial Autonomous BRT: 18-meter articulated autonomous electric buses running on exclusive rights-of-way with 3–5 minute headways along primary avenues.
  • 2
    On-Demand Suburban Feeders: 8–12 passenger autonomous pods dynamically grouping riders within suburban radii and channeling them into primary BRT stations.
  • 3
    V2X Dedicated Signal Preemption: Connected signals prioritize autonomous public transit at all grade crossings, guaranteeing predictable door-to-door transit times.
Pillar 2: Freight Consolidation

Multi-Carrier Urban Consolidation Centres (UCC)

Halting redundant runs where multiple proprietary couriers traverse the same inner-city street simultaneously by transitioning to shared local consolidation hubs.

  • 1
    Perimeter Transshipment Terminals: Regional couriers deposit city-bound goods at ring-road facilities rather than entering inner urban grids.
  • 2
    Single-Fleet Route Optimization: Shipments are sorted by neighborhood sector and delivered via shared e-cargo vehicles and autonomous delivery pods.
  • 3
    Universal Open-Access Locker Banks: Standardized, carrier-neutral smart locker banks deployed within 300 meters of all residential high-density complexes.

Phased Implementation Roadmap

A progressive 36-month transformation designed to balance regulatory alignment, fleet procurement, and carrier cooperation.

Phase 1 • Months 01 – 12

Regulatory Framework, Smart Lockers & Pilot UCC

Pass municipal ordinances establishing low-emission freight zones and dynamic curb pricing. Commission the first municipal Urban Consolidation Centre (UCC) at an outer ring interchange. Deploy 250+ carrier-agnostic lockers across rapid rail and suburban bus stations.

Phase 2 • Months 13 – 24

Autonomous Bus Corridors & Feeder Pod Deployment

Reallocate inner roadway lanes into dedicated autonomous transit rights-of-way. Introduce on-demand 12-passenger autonomous pods in two suburban feeder communities to funnel commuters into rail and BRT backbones.

Phase 3 • Months 25 – 36

Full Network Integration & Off-Peak Autonomous Logistics

Mandate inner-city participation in multi-carrier micro-consolidation for commercial deliveries. Implement off-peak autonomous freight shuttling between logistics hubs and neighborhood distribution points between 22:00 and 05:00.
